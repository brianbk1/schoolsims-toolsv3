Plan your outbound week

About 10 minutes each Monday. Work through the four steps in order; your settings and exclusions stay in this browser for next time.

  1. Pipeline target. Enter what you have closed this year and your open pipeline. You'll get how many companies to work each day.
  2. HubSpot export. Open the SchoolSims contact list in HubSpot, export it to CSV, and upload the file here.
  3. Review accounts. Uncheck anything you shouldn't call, like an account in a renewal conversation.
  4. Weekly plan. Follow the day's schedule, tap a contact for scripts, and download the .xlsx.
Step 1

How much outbound do you need?

Enter your goal, what you have already closed this year, and your current open pipeline. Closed-won deals come off the goal first, then the planner works back through close rate, a 90-day cycle and slow months to set how many companies to work each day.

Where to find these numbers: open your pipeline report in Salesforce. Use its total for Current open pipeline, and its deal amounts to set Average new deal. For Closed won so far this year, filter the same report to Closed Won with a close date this calendar year.

Step 2

Download the contact list from HubSpot

  1. Open the list in HubSpot. Log in if it asks.
    Open the HubSpot listhttps://app.hubspot.com/contacts/5514837/objectLists/13913/filters
  2. Click Export (top right of the list). Choose CSV and keep the columns in the current view. They already include the company fields.
  3. HubSpot emails you a download link. Download the file.
  4. Upload it here. Click Choose CSV file below, or drag the file onto the box. It's usually named like higher-ed-engagement-for-ai.csv.
